🏆 GIH OPEN

The Swedish School of Sport and Health Sciences annual Tennis tournament

How to Use

▼
  • Round 1 pairings are automatically set (1 vs 2, 3 vs 4, etc.)
  • Enter match scores (0-6 points per player) in each round
  • Standings update automatically as you enter scores
  • Click "Generate Round X" to create pairings based on current standings
  • Swiss system pairs players with similar points (avoiding repeat matchups)
  • After 5 rounds, the top 4 players advance to the semifinals
  • Winners of semifinals meet in the final to crown the champion!
